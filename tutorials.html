VPN SETUP TUTORIALS

Watch video guides and follow step-by-step instructions to configure your VPN application.

1. Setting Up Exotic Tunnel & Exotic Fast VIP

  1. Launch the app and open the side menu.
  2. Select Account Credentials and enter your Username & Password.
  3. Select an active server location and tap CONNECT.

2. Setting Up HTTP Custom

  1. Open HTTP Custom and tap the + (Plus) floating button.
  2. Select Open Config and import your downloaded `.hc` file.
  3. Check Use Payload / SSH and tap CONNECT.
